# A publication surface of its own

Documentation and publishing can share a Router without sharing the same
reading experience. The blog keeps its own archive, search, taxonomy, RSS,
sharing actions, and related reading while remaining part of the same
application.

That gives a project one source of truth for content and a clear place for
long-form updates, release notes, and technical writing.
